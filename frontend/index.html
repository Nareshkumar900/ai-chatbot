System Architecture & Medical Data Isolation

The AI Medical Doctor–Patient Management System is engineered to meet rigorous healthcare data governance standards, addressing unauthorized cross-doctor access, data custodianship, and safe AI assistance.

Strict Doctor Data Isolation

A doctor can only view records for patients they directly registered or for whom the patient has granted explicit consent. Any unauthorized attempt triggers an immediate 403 Forbidden and is flagged in the security audit log.

Unique Patient Medical IDs

Government identifiers (e.g. Aadhaar, SSN) are never exposed. Instead, cryptographically randomized Medical IDs (e.g. MED-IND-2026-8F92K1) are used for consent-based access requests.

Expiration Monitoring Engine

An automated background process periodically evaluates certificates against review dates, sending proactive in-app notifications to both the patient and their attending physician.

Medical AI Safety Guardrails

The AI Assistant detects emergency situations immediately, enforces medical disclaimers, never claims to be a doctor, and never prescribes medication without licensed physician validation.
